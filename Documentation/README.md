# 📚 Hardware Documentation, Manuals & Bill of Materials (BOM)

This directory contains complete technical documentation, step-by-step physical assembly guides, clearance specs, and component sourcing lists for our National Champion RoboCup autonomous soccer robot.

---

## Master Documents Reference

The following PDF guides and specification sheets are hosted in this folder:

1. **`Assembly_Guide.pdf`** — Step-by-step hardware assembly manual featuring exploded view diagrams, screw torque recommendations, and clearance checks.
2. **`Full_BOM_Sourcing_List.csv`** — Complete bill of materials listing exact part numbers, dimensions, material choices, and mass distribution.
3. **`RoboCup_Rulebook_Compliance.pdf`** — Technical verification document detailing compliance with official RoboCup Junior Soccer dimensional limits (cylinder constraint), mass limits, and safety regulations.
4. **`Field_Maintenance_and_Safety.pdf`** — Operational safety protocols, emergency repair workflows, and battery handling procedures between competition matches.

---

## Mechanical Constraints & Regulatory Compliance

All mechanical modules documented in this repository adhere strictly to **RoboCup Junior Soccer** official standards:
- **Footprint:** Designed to fit within a **220 mm diameter cylinder** (height limit: **220 mm**).
- **Weight Budget:** Rigorously optimized chassis structures to stay safely under competition mass thresholds.
- **Safety Integration:** Rounded physical edges, fully enclosed drive belts/gears, and secure LiPo battery containment.
